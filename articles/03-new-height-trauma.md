# 03 // NEW HEIGHT TRAUMA

Elevation changes the equation. When you step beyond standard thresholds, your sensory system recalibrates or overloads under the sheer scale of the drop.

Trauma at new heights isn't merely fear; it's a spatial disorientation where reference points dissolve and your internal balance must rebuild from the ground up.
