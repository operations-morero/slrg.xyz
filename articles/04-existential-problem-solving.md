# 04 // EXISTENTIAL PROBLEM SOLVING

When complex questions have no obvious answers, cognitive looping begins. The brain searches for clean logic in a space defined by uncertainty.

Existential problem solving requires stripping away superfluous variables. You don't solve the entire abyss—you anchor one structural beam at a time until the foundation holds.
