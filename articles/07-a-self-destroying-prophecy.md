# 07 // A SELF-DESTROYING PROPHECY

A self-fulfilling prophecy creates the outcome it expects; a self-destroying prophecy anticipates the disaster so intensely that it forces the architecture to adapt and nullify the threat before impact.

When you spend your life managing hyper-vigilance and pattern recognition, you don't wait for systems to fail—you map every fault line in advance and build the counter-weights into the bedrock.
