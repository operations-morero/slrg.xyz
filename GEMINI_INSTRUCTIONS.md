### How to Write on Mobile with Gemini:

1. In your Gemini chat, after drafting your essay, ask Gemini:
   > *"Format this for my website SLRG.XYZ and put it in a single markdown code block."*

2. Tap **Copy** on the code block.

3. Open your Google Drive app, go to **`slrg`**, tap the existing article file (e.g. `03-new-height-trauma.md` or create a new `.md` file) and paste the text!

---

### Markdown Template:

```markdown
---
id: "03"
title: "03 // YOUR ARTICLE TITLE HERE"
date: "2026.09.20"
timestamp: "2026-09-20T12:00:00Z"
tags:
  - "#SYSTEMS"
  - "#NEURODIVERGENT"
summary: "A crisp 1-2 sentence punchy summary of the core thesis."
source_link: "https://optional-link.com"
source_title: "READ ORIGINAL SOURCE"
---

Your first paragraph here.

Your second paragraph here with blank line separation.
```

*(Note: If you ever write an article without frontmatter headers, the build system will automatically extract the title and summary for you!)*
